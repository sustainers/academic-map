# Institutional RSE Networking Affinity Group

- **Website:** [Institutional RSE Networking](https://us-rse.org/ag-institutional-rse-networking/)
- **Parent organization:** [US-RSE](../organizations/usrse.md)
- **Join:** [US-RSE Slack channel](https://usrse.slack.com/archives/C07U53KED19)

## Overview

An affinity group of the US Research Software Engineer Association that "connects Research Software Engineers (RSEs) who support or aspire to develop RSE communities within their organizations." It focuses on connecting RSEs across departments, building sustainable local communities, and sharing community leadership practices. The group holds regular meetups and welcomes participants at any stage of building an RSE community.
