# Networking Groups

Networking groups, working groups, and affinity groups bring practitioners from different institutions together to share what works. They usually have no staff or budget of their own, and are often part of a larger organization.

## What belongs here

A group is listed here if:

- Its main purpose is connecting people across institutions, rather than producing software or funding work.
- It is relevant to open source, research software, or open practices in academia.
- It has a public page and a way for new people to join, such as a mailing list, Slack channel, or regular meetings.

Groups that are part of a larger organization are listed here only if they have their own public page and a focus distinct from their parent. Otherwise, they are mentioned on the parent organization's page. Membership communities with their own governance or funding, such as [CURIOSS](../organizations/curioss.md), are listed under [Organizations](../organizations/index.md).
