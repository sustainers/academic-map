# Open Source for Science Fund

- **Website:** [Open Source for Science Fund](https://os4science.org/)
- **Host:** [Renaissance Philanthropy](https://www.renaissancephilanthropy.org/)
- **Director:** Dario Taraborelli

## Overview

The Open Source for Science Fund (OS4Science) describes itself as "a multi-donor fund to sustain and evolve the open source software infrastructure for data-intensive research and AI-enabled discovery." Renaissance Philanthropy hosts it. It [launched in May 2026](https://os4science.org/news/open-source-for-science-fund-launch/) with $20 million in anchor funding from Biohub and Wellcome. The Kavli Foundation, the [Research Software Alliance](../organizations/research-software-alliance.md), and the NIH Office of Data Science Strategy also support it, and Hugging Face has partnered with it.

The fund continues the [Chan Zuckerberg Initiative](./czi.md)'s Essential Open Source Software for Science (EOSS) program, which deployed $58 million across more than 230 projects over six cycles. The EOSS team designed the fund. Its director, Dario Taraborelli, previously led open science funding at CZI.

## Funding

Its first call, Open Source for the Life Sciences (2026), offered grants of up to $250,000 for domain-specific tools, and up to $1,000,000 for foundational libraries and ecosystem initiatives. That call has closed. Future calls will be listed on its [funding opportunities page](https://os4science.org/apply-for-funding/).
