# Free Software Foundation (FSF)

- **Website:** [Free Software Foundation (FSF)](https://www.fsf.org/)

## Overview

The Free Software Foundation (FSF) is a non-profit organization founded in 1985, dedicated to promoting computer user freedom by defending the rights of software users. It advocates for the freedom to use, study, copy, modify, and redistribute computer software.

It is heavily linked to [Richard Stallman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Stallman), who resigned from MIT during the Epstein scandal. He was later re-admitted to the board.
