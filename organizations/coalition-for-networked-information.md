# Coalition for Networked Information (CNI)

- **Website:** [CNI](https://www.cni.org/)

## Overview

The Coalition for Networked Information (CNI) is a joint program of the [Association of Research Libraries](https://www.arl.org/) (ARL) and [EDUCAUSE](https://www.educause.edu/) that promotes the use of networked information technology to advance scholarly communication and research. It was founded in 1990 by ARL and the two organizations that later merged to form EDUCAUSE.

CNI has over 200 member organizations from higher education, libraries, publishing, and information technology. Its membership meetings, held each spring and fall, bring research libraries and campus IT groups together to share their work.
