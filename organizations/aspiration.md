# Aspiration

- **Website:** [Aspiration](https://aspirationtech.org/)

## Overview

Aspiration describes itself as "a values-driven nonprofit technology organization." Based in San Francisco, it offers fiscal sponsorship to mission-aligned technology, data, and digital rights projects, and services for nonprofits, free and open source projects, and funders. It also runs convenings such as the Nonprofit Software Development Summit, along with trainings and leadership cohorts.

Aspiration is one of the partners behind the [Digital Infrastructure Insights Fund](../funders/diif.md).
