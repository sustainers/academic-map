# Institute for Research Software

- [Website](https://www.software.ac.uk/)

The Institute for Research Software was founded in 2010 as the Software Sustainability Institute (SSI), the first organisation dedicated to improving software in research. It took its current name in 2026. It works to make research software reusable, sustainable and valuable, and to support and gain recognition for the people who create, maintain and use it.

The Institute is delivered through a partnership between EPCC at the University of Edinburgh, the University of Manchester, and the University of Southampton. Its core funding for 2024 to 2028 comes from UKRI through the Digital Research Infrastructure programme, led by the Arts and Humanities Research Council.
