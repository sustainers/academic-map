# Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS)

*Homepage*: [https://joss.theoj.org/](https://joss.theoj.org/)

The Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS) publishes articles about research software. For most researchers papers and not software are the currency of academic research and citations are required for advancement; JOSS fills this need for research software engineers.

JOSS is an academic journal (ISSN 2475-9066) with a formal peer review process that is designed to improve the quality of the software submitted. JOSS does not charge article processing charges or subscription fees.
