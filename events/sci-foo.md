# Sci Foo

- **Website:** [Sci Foo](https://www.digital-science.com/resource/scifoo/)
- **When:** Every summer
- **Where:** Traditionally at Google in California; held in Cambridge, UK, in 2024

## Overview

Science Foo Camp (Sci Foo) is an invitation-only unconference for scientists, technologists, and policymakers. Digital Science, Google, and O'Reilly Media have run it since 2006, together with Nature. About 300 people attend, and they set the agenda themselves during the event. "Foo" stands for "Friends of O'Reilly."

[Where do ideas come from?](https://www.digital-science.com/tldr/article/where-do-ideas-come-from/) is one of its organizers' account of the event.
