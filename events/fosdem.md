# FOSDEM

- **Website:** [FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/)
- **When:** A weekend in late January or early February
- **Where:** Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB) Solbosch campus, Brussels, Belgium

## Overview

FOSDEM describes itself as "a two-day event organised by volunteers to promote the widespread use of free and open source software." Attendance is free. It began in 2000 as OSDEM, and took its current name the following year. Most talks take place in developer rooms ("devrooms"), each run by a community around a single topic.
