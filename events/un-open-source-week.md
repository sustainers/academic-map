# UN Open Source Week

- **Website:** [UN Open Source Week](https://www.unopensource.org/)
- **When:** Every June, since 2025
- **Where:** United Nations Headquarters, New York, USA

## Overview

UN Open Source Week describes itself as "the premier global forum for advancing open source collaboration." The UN Office for Digital and Emerging Technologies (ODET) and the UN Office of Information and Communications Technology (OICT) co-host it. It brings together governments, UN agencies, companies, civil society, and technical communities, and links open source work to the Sustainable Development Goals and the Global Digital Compact.

The 2026 edition included an "OSPOs for Good" day on the role of Open Source Program Offices.
