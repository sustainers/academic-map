# University of Pennsylvania

- *OSPO*: No

## General Description

The University of Pennsylvania (UPenn) does not have an Open Source Program Office. Open source work happens in individual units, including:

- **Linguistic Data Consortium (LDC):** distributes [language resources](https://www.ldc.upenn.edu/language-resources) for research in human language technology.
- **GitHub Campus Program:** gives the UPenn community access to GitHub Enterprise; many university projects are published under the [UPenn GitHub organization](https://github.com/upenn).
- **Penn Libraries:** publishes its software on [GitHub](https://github.com/upenn-libraries).
